## Meaning and limits

The mechanism has research value as an auditable link from task-specific arm posture/path constraints to docking feasibility and then to actual base-navigation cost, with full coverage and minimum docking count held fixed. The profile support counts differ substantially (1,420 / 472 / 493 of 2,037 pairs), while the stop count remains 16 in this scene. Do not claim that these posture constraints necessarily increase the stop count. The three profiles also differ in motion/path requirements and are not a clean strictly nested orientation-only experiment.

A single deterministic scene does not establish general superiority, statistical significance, algorithm novelty, scaling complexity or runtime advantage. The 54 targeted tests are not a full repository suite. Callback counts can include cross-method memo hits; the 2,108-entry lazy scalar memo excludes other Stage 3 matrix/stitch queries. Selection/search timings exclude preprocessing, IK and final reconstruction, and cache temperature varies with fixed run order. The final analysis corrected those labels and makes no total-cost speedup claim.

No arm/self/inter-arm collision, inter-task arm transition, continuous sampled-interval swept motion, calibrated hardware, contact force or physical grasp/wipe success is established. Sampled physical base separation is the independent route-check scope. These are limits of the supported claim, not reasons to withhold the completed bounded result.
