RCH-MT-MMP maps tabletop tasks to reachable docking-pose coverage sets before global routing. Multi-docking solves task distributions that one pose cannot cover. The current draft is based on known maps, simplified reachability, and offline planning; future work should add dynamics, online perception, replanning, and real-robot validation.
